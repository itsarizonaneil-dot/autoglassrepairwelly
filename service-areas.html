Service Areas — Wellington Region

Wellington City

Wellington's steep streets, roadworks and gusty southerlies keep a steady supply of grit and debris in the air, and on-street parking in the inner suburbs means side windows can be smashed in break-ins. Whether you need a quick chip repair or an urgent glass replacement after an overnight incident, give us a call and we'll get onto it straight away.

Lower Hutt & Wainuiomata

Heavy traffic on State Highway 2 and the Hutt Road, plus the climb over the Wainuiomata Hill, means plenty of stone chips from trucks and roadworks. If you've noticed a chip after a commute down the valley, it's worth getting it looked at before it spreads.

Porirua

Porirua sits at the start of the run north on the motorway, where higher speeds mean any loose stone that gets flicked up hits harder. If you've picked up a chip on the way in or out of the city, send us the details and we'll take a look.

Kāpiti Coast

Long commutes on the expressway and the coastal weather make the Kāpiti Coast tough on windscreens, and many drivers up here would rather not lose a day driving into town. Ask about a mobile visit when you enquire and we'll bring the repair to you.

Upper Hutt

Cold, frosty mornings in Upper Hutt are a common cause of cracked windscreens, especially when someone pours hot water on the ice. Don't do that: use a de-icer or let the demister do its job. If a crack has appeared, or you're heading over the Remutaka Hill and want it sorted first, get in touch.
