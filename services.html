Auto Glass Services, Wellington

From a quick stone chip repair to a full windscreen replacement, our team looks after all your auto glass needs across the Wellington region.

Not sure which service you need? Have a look through the options below, or send us a few details and we'll point you to the right fix and give you a free quote.

Auto glass & Windscreen Repair

A windscreen chip can happen unexpectedly sometimes without you noticing straight away. The longer it’s left, it can grow and turn into something much bigger. When it’s seen to early, a repair can often still be possible, and we’d always prefer to repair the windscreen before considering a windscreen replacement.

Windscreen Replacement

A new windscreen restores the strength and brings back a clear view of the road your car was built with, even when the damage is too extensive to repair. We know it’s an inconvenience, so our team works efficiently to fit the job around you, making it one less thing to worry over.

Learn More

Side mirror Repair and Replacement

A cracked or loose side mirror is hard to ignore, especially in traffic or when manoeuvring into a tricky park. Often a side mirror glass replacement is quicker and simpler than a whole new mirror. We can quickly tell what’s needed for your damaged side mirror even from a description of the damage and the make of your car. Having a clear view beside and from behind makes a bigger difference than people expect.

Learn More

Sunroof Glass Repair and Replacement

A damaged sunroof and small leaks can lead to damp seats and that musty smell over time. It won’t always show up as a chip or crack and the sunroof seal is what needs attention instead. Our team checks which it is so you’re not left wondering what is actually needed. Getting that answer early saves guessing and keeps the inside of your car dry.

Learn More

Mobile auto glass repair

Plenty of glass repairs can be done right where you are, at home or at work. It means fitting around your day, with no added trip to a workshop and no time spent waiting. For a lot of people, that convenience is what turns “I’ll get to it eventually” into getting it sorted this week.
