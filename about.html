What We Do

A connection service, not a glass shop

Auto Glass Repair Welly exists to make one thing simple: getting damaged auto glass sorted in the Wellington region. We take your enquiry and pass it to a local auto glass specialist, who inspects the damage, gives you a quote, and carries out the repair or replacement.

We're not a glass workshop ourselves, and we don't repair or fit glass. We think you should always know exactly who's working on your vehicle, so we're upfront about that from the start.
