About Wellington Auto Glass Repair Pros

Getting a windscreen chip repaired or damaged glass replaced is key to staying safe on the road, and you want to know the auto glass repair specialist you choose in Wellington will take care of all the work that needs to be done. That’s exactly what our team is here to do. At Wellington Auto Glass Repair Pros, we’re the local auto glass repair experts near you that you can rely on for your vehicle. Our focus is always on finding you a solution that’s timely and effective. We make sure the glass we fit is done right the first time, without cutting corners and without over engineering the job when a simple fix will do. We still believe in doing every job properly, and we use proven methods that let us work with precision, accuracy and care.

We start by working out exactly what the problem is, and then we give you a solution that lasts. Our goal is to get every driver’s glass repaired or replaced quickly, so you can get on with your day, because there’s always another drive coming up.

Our team is committed to high-quality results, and the work we do for you is built to last for years. Bringing your vehicle’s glass back to a safe standard is an important step for every driver, and the faster it’s done, the sooner you’re back on the road. Whether you drive a car, SUV, ute or van, we’ll take care of it for you.
