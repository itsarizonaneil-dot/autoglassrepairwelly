Windscreen Replacement, Wellington

When repair isn't enough, a full replacement keeps you safe and legal. Here's what's involved and how to get a quote.

Your windscreen does more than keep the wind out. It's bonded to the body of the vehicle, helps support the roof in a rollover, and in many cars it's the surface the passenger airbag pushes against when it deploys. That's why a badly damaged windscreen is a safety problem, not just a cosmetic one.

Replacement is usually needed when damage is large, reaches the edge of the glass, sits right in the driver's line of sight, or has spread into a long crack. Our team will remove the old glass, prepare the frame, and bond in the new windscreen with automotive adhesive.

Many newer vehicles also have cameras and sensors behind the windscreen for lane assistance or automatic braking. After a replacement these systems may need recalibrating, so let us know your vehicle's make, model and year when you enquire and we'll plan for it. If you're claiming through insurance, check your policy first — our FAQ has the basics.

What to expect

  • We assess the damage and quote before any work starts
  • We remove the damaged glass and clean the frame
  • We bond in the new windscreen with automotive adhesive
  • We tell you how long to wait before driving, as the adhesive needs time to cure
  • Camera or sensor recalibration if your vehicle needs it, arranged as part of the job

Exact steps, timing and costs are confirmed when we quote your job.
