Auto Glass Repair, Windscreen Replacement & More Across Wellington

We specialise in auto glass repair and windscreen replacement for drivers across Wellington, Lower Hutt, Porirua, Kāpiti Coast and Upper Hutt. Whether it's a stone chip from the motorway, a crack that's started creeping across your windscreen, or a side window smashed overnight, our team is well equipped to get it sorted and have you back on the road without the runaround.

Glass damage never turns up at a convenient time, and it's a safety issue as much as an annoyance. A windscreen is part of your vehicle's structure and plays a role in how airbags deploy, so we don't believe in leaving it. Tell us what's happened and we'll take care of the rest.

About Us

Our team is focused on doing the job properly the first time. We know sorting out car glass can be a hassle, especially if you're not sure whether to repair or replace, or what your insurer will cover, so we keep things simple and honest. We're always happy to answer questions, point you in the right direction, and look after your vehicle from start to finish.
