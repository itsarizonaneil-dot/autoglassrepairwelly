A small chip today can be a full replacement next week

Wellington's wind, temperature swings and flicked-up road grit can turn a tiny chip into a long crack in a matter of days. Caught early, many chips can be repaired instead of replaced. Damage in the driver's line of sight can also put your Warrant of Fitness at risk, so it's worth getting it looked at sooner rather than later.

Auto Glass Repair, Windscreen Replacement & More Across Wellington

We help drivers across Wellington, Lower Hutt, Porirua, Kāpiti Coast and Upper Hutt get damaged glass sorted without the runaround. Whether it's a stone chip on the motorway, a crack that's started creeping across the windscreen, or a side window smashed overnight, send us the details and we'll pass them to a local auto glass specialist who can quote the job and get you back on the road.

Glass damage never turns up at a convenient time, and it's a safety issue as much as an annoyance. A windscreen is part of your vehicle's structure and plays a role in how airbags deploy, so we keep the process short: tell us what's wrong, and we'll make the connection.
