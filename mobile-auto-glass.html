Mobile Auto Glass, Wellington

Too busy to drop the car off? Our mobile service can come to you at home or at work.

Many auto glass jobs, particularly chip repairs and a lot of replacements, can be done on-site by a mobile technician. That means no dropping the car off and no finding a lift home — we come to you and get on with it.

Whether a mobile visit suits your job depends on the type of work, the weather, and where you are. Rain, wind and dust don't mix well with adhesives and resin, and some vehicles need equipment or recalibration that's easier done in a workshop. We'll let you know the best option when we quote.

When you enquire, mention that you'd like a mobile visit, along with your suburb and what's damaged, and we'll take it from there.

To help a mobile visit go smoothly

  • Park somewhere with a bit of room around the vehicle
  • A flat, sheltered spot such as a garage or carport helps, especially in wet weather
  • Tell us the make, model and year of your vehicle
  • Let us know if the glass has cameras or sensors fitted
  • Leave your best contact number so we can confirm timing

Mobile availability varies by area and job — we'll confirm what's possible when we get in touch.
