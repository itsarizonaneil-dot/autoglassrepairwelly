Windscreen Chip & Crack Repair, Wellington

Caught a stone chip? A quick repair now can save you a full windscreen later. Here's what we can fix and how to get it done.

Windscreen repair fills a chip or short crack with a clear resin that's cured in place. It restores much of the glass's strength, stops the damage from spreading, and makes the blemish far less noticeable — and it's usually done in a fraction of the time a replacement takes.

The catch is that time matters. Dirt and moisture work their way into a chip, and a day of heat, cold or bumpy driving can send a crack racing across the glass. Once that happens, repair is often no longer an option and you're looking at a replacement.

Not everything can be repaired, and we'll always tell you honestly once we've seen the damage. Even so, it's always worth getting it checked — if it does need replacing, our replacement service has you covered.

What can usually be repaired

  • Small stone chips, such as bullseyes, star breaks and combination breaks
  • Short cracks that haven't reached the edge of the glass
  • Damage that's still clean and dry, before dirt gets in
  • Chips outside the driver's direct line of sight

This is a general guide only. We'll confirm whether a repair is suitable once we've inspected the damage.
